03 / THE BIGGER PICTURE

Driven by better questions.

Good science starts
with a human problem.

How can food reach the people who need it most? How can organizations make better decisions with limited resources? These are the questions that guide my work.

My research brings mathematical optimization, statistical learning, and simulation together to study food-bank networks, equitable access, and collaboration in humanitarian supply chains.

I’m an Applied Scientist at Amazon in Bellevue, Washington. My academic journey connects industrial and production engineering at BUET with doctoral research at NC State. Along the way, I’ve also taught engineering and explored how research translates into practical decisions.
